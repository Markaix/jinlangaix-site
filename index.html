Quality

Most of the work in this trade is paperwork that fails an audit.

The certificate is issued under our name and our seal, not forwarded as a PDF from a plant. The analytical methods are compiled by us, which is why they are bilingual and why we can explain any line on the sheet when your QA team asks.

Where your market or your customer requires a report from a laboratory that is not the manufacturer, testing is arranged through a government-accredited laboratory in Changsha. Give us the limits, we send the sample, and the report travels with the lot.

How lots are released

With every lot
  • Certificate of Analysis EN Assay, heavy metals, microbiology, moisture, ash, lot number, manufacturing and report dates
  • Specification sheet EN The limits the lot was released against, for registration and your customer's file
  • Analytical method EN / 中文 The method used for each test item, item by item
  • Certificate of Origin EN Issued for customs clearance
  • Material Safety Data Sheet On request Supplied with the quotation when the product requires it
