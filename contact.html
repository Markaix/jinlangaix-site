What to send

A quotation is built from the formula or the specification, so the more precise the first message, the shorter the exchange.

  • Single material. Product name, assay, and the limits your market requires.
  • Blend. The components, the percentage or the dose of each, the dosage form, and the carrier if you have a preference.
  • Either way. The annual volume, and the market the finished product is sold in.

If something in that list is still open, write anyway and say so. We would rather work it out with you than wait for a complete brief.

Send a specification that keeps failing on arrival, even if we never trade. There is a good chance we can tell you why it is failing.
