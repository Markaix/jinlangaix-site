We stand behind the drum, not in front of it.

Hunan Jinlang Aix Bio-Tech Inc., founded April 2014. Botanical extracts, nucleotide ingredients and formulated blends, shipped in 25 kg drums and in sample quantities.

The role changes with the order. Some buyers want a plant and a capacity number. Some want the specification settled before anyone talks price. Some want a report from a laboratory that is not the maker. Some want a blend costed before they commit to a formula. All four come off the same desk. The plant changes with the product; the name on the certificate does not.

What that means in practice

Sourcing and qualifying the plants. Agreeing the specification with the customer before anything is quoted. Arranging the testing, writing the certificate, and answering for the lot after it has shipped. When something is wrong, the call comes to us, not to a plant office that has never heard of your order.

Our name is not on the building. It is on the specification, the certificate of analysis and the certificate of origin — the three documents that decide whether a container clears and whether your QA signs the lot off. Some buyers want to shake hands with a plant manager. We are the part that is still there afterwards: the file, the testing, the call in year three.

Why the company exists

Because the same thing kept happening. A buyer asks for a specification and gets a price instead. Or gets a specification with nothing behind it: a number on a page, no method, no lot, no one to call.

So the order of work here is specification, then price, then the documents your QA team can actually file. It is slower at the start, and it is the reason customers stay.
